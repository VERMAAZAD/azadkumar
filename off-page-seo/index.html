What off-page SEO includes

Off-page SEO covers everything that happens outside your website to build trust: backlinks, brand mentions and your wider online reputation. Search engines treat a link from a relevant, respected site as a vote of confidence, and quality and relevance beat sheer volume every time.

Backlink audit

Reviewing your current links to separate valuable ones from low-quality or risky ones.

Competitor link gaps

Finding sites that link to competitors but not to you, the most realistic opportunities.

Guest posting

Writing genuinely useful articles for relevant publications in your niche.

Broken-link building

Finding dead links on good sites and offering your page as a working replacement.

Link reclamation

Turning unlinked brand mentions and lost links into live backlinks.

Brand mentions

Earning citations and coverage through useful resources and expert commentary.

What a good link looks like

I judge each opportunity against these points before outreach begins.

  • The site is topically relevant to yours
  • It has real readers and genuine editorial standards
  • The link sits naturally inside useful content
  • The anchor text looks natural, not over-optimized
  • The site is not built to sell links or run a link farm
  • The link would still make sense if search engines did not exist

My link-building process

  1. Audit

    Backlink profile and competitor analysis with Ahrefs, Semrush and Moz.

  2. Strategy

    A plan that matches tactics to your niche and to the pages that need authority.

  3. Outreach

    Personalized emails to relevant editors and site owners, with a clear reason to link.

  4. Placement

    Guest posts and resources are published on vetted sites, and every link is recorded.

  5. Monitor

    I track new and lost links, authority trends and referral traffic in regular reports.

Why I avoid risky tactics

Link schemes such as buying links in bulk, private blog networks and automated spam can trigger manual actions or algorithmic demotion, and recovery is slow and costly. A smaller number of relevant links compounds safely, which is the approach I take.

Frequently asked questions

How many backlinks do I need?

There is no fixed number. It depends on your competitors and niche, so I benchmark against the sites ranking above you to set a realistic target.

Do you guarantee links or rankings?

I cannot guarantee outcomes because publishers decide independently. What I do commit to is relevant outreach, transparent reporting and no spammy tactics.

How long does link building take to work?

Links usually need weeks or months to influence rankings, and the effect builds gradually.

Can you fix a toxic backlink profile?

I can audit it, identify risky links and advise on cleanup, including a disavow file where it is genuinely warranted.
