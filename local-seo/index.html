How local search rankings work

Google describes three main local ranking factors: relevance (how well your profile matches the search), distance (how close you are to the searcher) and prominence (how well known and trusted you are). You cannot change distance, but you can strengthen relevance and prominence, which is where local SEO work focuses.

Reviews and reputation

Reviews influence both rankings and conversions. I help you set up an ethical review process: asking real customers, never buying reviews or gating them, which breaks Google’s policies, and replying thoughtfully to both praise and criticism.

Service-area and multi-location businesses

If you travel to customers or operate from several branches, each needs the right setup: service areas defined correctly, one profile per genuine location and dedicated pages that show real local value.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a physical address?

Not always. Service-area businesses can hide their address in Google Business Profile, but they must still follow Google’s guidelines.

How long does local SEO take?

Profile improvements can help within weeks, while competitive markets may take a few months to build prominence.

Does local SEO work for online-only businesses?

It mainly suits businesses that serve a physical area. Online-only businesses usually benefit more from technical, on-page and link-building work.

Can you help in the USA, UK, Canada and Australia?

Yes. Each country has its own directories, address formats and search habits, which I account for.
